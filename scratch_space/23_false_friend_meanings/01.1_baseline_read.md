# Baseline read of `means`, before the review

The 500 false-friend hints of the most frequent pt trap cards (known languages en and it), read
on the current corpus with phase 1's code, before any pin of this review.
The list is [`01.1_baseline_500.jsonl`](01.1_baseline_500.jsonl), in card frequency order;
phase 5 re-reads the same rows.

The question for each hint: is the first word of `means` what the look-alike mainly means to
a speaker of its language today (the word acerta would show as the trap)?
Borderline picks that a learner would still recognise (it *sede* "site", it *ingresso* "hall")
count as right.

## Numbers

Whole corpus: 836 pt trap cards, 973 false-friend hints.

| hints with en `means` / it `means` | en hint | it hint |
| --- | --- | --- |
| full, a word | 217 | 220 |
| full, empty | 33 | 0 |
| partial, a word | 200 | 252 |
| partial, empty | 51 | 0 |

Every it word's best sense has an en word; 84 en hints (9%) have no it word for theirs.

The 500 read:

| | en | it | all |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| right | 206 | 215 | 421 |
| wrong | 12 | 30 | 42 |
| empty | 37 | 0 | 37 |

Of the 463 with a word, 42 are wrong (9%): 5% of the en ones, 12% of the it ones.
The it share fits the cause in [`00_start.md`](00_start.md): it senses tie on frequency, so
commonness picks.
