# Phase 3 - Pilot

## Overview

One batch of 100 words and the gold set, read and measured before the rest.
Context: [`00_start.md`](00_start.md) Q4, [`02_harness.md`](02_harness.md).

## Plan

- The usage reading (session and weekly) before and after, so the rest can be projected.
- One batch of 100 by a subagent through the skill; the gold set through the same path.
- My read of all 100: agreement, and where the rulebook was unclear.
- Rulebook or harness fixes proposed to the user before the rest; a fix that changes the
  rulebook re-serves the pilot words whose label it would change.

## Done when

- Agreement on the gold set and on my read of the pilot logged in `tracking.md`, with the usage
  share; the user agrees the rest.

## What the implementation found

- Batch `f0001` (the 30 gold words and the next 70 by frequency), one subagent through the skill:
  74 `k`, 24 `p`, 2 `m` (it *sito* has no "website" sense, en *based* no "based on"), 13 edge
  cases; applied on the first try.
- Gold: 30 of 30 agree.
- My read of the other 70: all acceptable. Close calls the reviewer flagged (en *point*, en
  *record*, it *ora*) go either way. en *come* shows *essere* and en *vice* *debolezza*: the
  right sense with a weak first word, the lead-word problem of `01.1_baseline_read.md`, which a
  pin cannot fix.
- No rulebook or harness fix needed.
- Usage: the subagent took about 90k tokens and 74 seconds; the session and weekly readings are
  the user's to take (not readable from here).
- The user's "go for all phases" (2026-11-25) stands as the go-ahead for the rest.
