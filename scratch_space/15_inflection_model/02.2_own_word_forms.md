# Forms that are also a word of their own

The 498 generated pt forms of the 2026-11-11 build that kaikki also gives a sense of their own
(00_start.md Q7 a: added as forms, listed here for a later review).
Most are participles used as adjectives (*abatido*, *aberta*) or verb forms with a colloquial
extra sense (*imagina* "no problem", *fechou* "OK", *valeu* "thanks"); some are nouns spelled like
a verb form (*choro*, *namoro*, *recreio*), where the form reading is the rarer one.
Listed by `02.1_check_forms.py` from the scratchpad kaikki index.
