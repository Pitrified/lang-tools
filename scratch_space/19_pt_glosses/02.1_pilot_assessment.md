# Pilot assessment

Batches `g0001` to `g0003`, 600 pt glosses, written on 2026-11-13 by three subagents in parallel
under the `gloss-write` skill, rules `gloss_translate/v3.jinja` (hash `7ce135836c6a`).
Every batch applied on its first try: no gloss failed the screen.
All 600 were read by the orchestrating session, each against its English gloss and both
languages' words (sheet: one line per item, `batch#n | English | en words | pt words || gloss`).

## Verdicts

| Verdict | Count | Share |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Right | 590 | 98.3% |
| Right, with a small flaw | 6 | 1.0% |
| Faithful to a broken English gloss, useless to a learner | 2 | 0.3% |
| Wrong | 2 | 0.3% |

**Wrong (corrected):**

- `g0001#112` wild strawberry: the English gloss in the corpus is only "Europe", and the gloss
  was "Europa". Corrected to "morangueiro silvestre da Europa".
- `g0003#162` *only* (adverb): the English "with nevertheless the final result" is a fragment of
  WordNet's gloss, and the gloss "com, no entanto, o resultado final de" says nothing.
  Corrected to "mas, no fim, com o resultado de que (chegou só para descobrir que...)".

**Faithful to a broken English gloss (corrected):**

- `g0001#11` Saints Peter and Paul: English "first celebrated in the 3rd century", gloss
  "celebrada pela primeira vez no século III". Corrected to "festa de São Pedro e São Paulo, em
  29 de junho, celebrada pela primeira vez no século III" (the date and the name are the
  concept's own words).
- `g0003#181` vernal equinox: English "March 21", gloss "21 de março". Corrected to
  "o equinócio de 21 de março".

All four through `gloss_write_cli correct`: accepted rows in `gloss_overrides.jsonl`.

**Small flaws (left as they are):**

- Adds a little the English does not say: `g0002#46` snort "expirando com força **pelo nariz**";
  `g0003#35` "tipo de pinça **cirúrgica**"; `g0003#145` French loaf "pão de **massa alongada** à
  moda francesa".
- Stiff wording: `g0001#8` "numa maneira de falar" (for "in a manner of speaking");
  `g0002#48` "com um movimento de bico" (for "with a picking motion").
- `g0001#172` *come back to* (*remoer*): "voltar sempre ao mesmo assunto" narrows the English to
  the sense the words mean; fine for a learner.

None of these misleads a learner; correcting them is not worth a pass.

## What the rules do well

The variety is consistently pt-BR (*usuário*, *esportiva*, *xícara*, *zíper*),
the register is a dictionary's, the short English glosses get short pt ones, and the domain
labels are kept and translated ("(lógica)", "(psicanálise)", "(Brasil)").
The subagents also used the English sense when the pt words disagreed with it, which is right:
the gloss defines the concept, not the words.

## Recommendation on the rules

They hold: keep v3 as is for the full run.
One change to the skill, not to the rules: the report should list, by item number, every item
whose English gloss looks truncated or is a fragment. Two of the four corrections above came
from what the subagents flagged on their own; making it a required report line costs nothing and
gives the sampled review its targets.

## Side finding: pt words linked to the wrong concept

Not this folder's problem, but the pilot is a random sample of OpenWordnet-PT's links on
concepts the old pt wordnet did not have, so it measures them.
About 20 of the 600 items (3%) have pt words that do not mean the concept, among them:
*inundar* on "stagger, astound", *estar* on "be felt in a certain way", *espadril* on a
ladder-back chair, *poupar* on "nickel-and-dime", *defemininizar* on "remove the ovaries",
*economicamente* on "thriftlessly", *Jersey* on Jersey City, *congressional* on
Congregational, *aguda* on "pitched", *salário* on payroll, *defensor* on "stalwart",
*entubar* on "trim with piping", *Tempo da Septuagésima* on Shrovetide, *atingir* on "pierce
with force", *difícil* on "with pain or bitterness", *enfurecido* on "very severe",
*levar* on "spend time".
Most are rare concepts, so few reach a card, and the sense review handles the frequent words.
Worth a line in `open_points.md`.
