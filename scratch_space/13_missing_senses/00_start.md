# Missing senses

## Origin

The card-sense review in [`12_card_senses`](../12_card_senses/00_start.md) keeps, pins or drops
the senses a word is linked to; it cannot add one.
Its re-list of the pt top 500 cards left about 16 doubtful cards, most because the right
meaning is not linked at all, and its drops removed nine lemmas whose only links were wrong.
Adding the links is a different job (a new curated input, not a review label), so it gets its
own folder.

## The words found so far (pt)

- Doubtful cards with no right sense: *sim* "yes", *além* "beyond", *última* "last", *seis*
  "six", *partes* "parts", *nomes* "names", *irmãos* "brothers", *índia* "India", *natal*
  "Christmas".
- Senses noted as missing during the review: *sede* "headquarters", *sentido* "direction".
- Lemmas lost to a correct drop, which a new link brings back (a drop removes one
  lemma-concept link, not the lemma): *vinte* "twenty", *cura* "cure", *campeonato*
  "tournament", *brasil* and *pernambuco* (the country, the state), *mano* "bro", *destaque*
  "prominence", *terras* "lands".
