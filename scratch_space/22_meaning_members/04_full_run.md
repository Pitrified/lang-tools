# Phase 4 - The rest, in waves

## Overview

The remaining member batches, about 72, in waves of 10 to 12 subagents, over several sessions.
Context: [`00_start.md`](00_start.md), depends on [`03_pilot.md`](03_pilot.md).

## Goals

1. Every card meaning with two or more pt words reviewed.
2. A usage reading after each wave; the next wave sized from it.

## Done when

- `status` shows no batch left; the verdict counts are logged.

## What the implementation found

- Stopped after wave 1 by the user's choice, on the measured cost: wave 1 and a re-check took
  about 2.2 session points a batch, so the other ~61 batches would have cost about 135 session
  and 19 weekly points. 1,705 of the 7,743 meanings are reviewed (the pilot and w0007 to
  w0018); the rest is parked in `open_points.md` as "Unreviewed card meanings".
- Wave 1 needed one rule fix and a re-check (silent consonants, regional spellings; w9002).
