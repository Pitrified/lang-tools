# Dataset card

What the lexical data is, where each part comes from, and how much of it is measured rather than
estimated.
The license and the attribution to give are in
[`DATA_LICENSE.md`](https://github.com/Pitrified/lang-tools/blob/main/DATA_LICENSE.md) at the
repo root: CC BY 4.0 for the data, with the French wordnet (WOLF, CeCILL-C) outside that grant.
The code is MIT (`LICENSE`).

The numbers below are from the build of 2026-11-13 (`data/lexicon/_build.json`); a rebuild
writes fresh ones there.

## What it holds

A concept-centric lexicon of five languages: concepts shared across languages through the
Collaborative Interlingual Index, the words of each language linked to them as senses, relations
between concepts and between senses, and look-alike pairs (false friends and cognates).
The model is described in [Lexicon](lexicon.md).

| | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| Concepts | 117,725 |
| Lemmas | 342,122 |
| Senses | 577,901 |
| Concept relations | 137,936 |
| Sense relations | 4,268 |
| Look-alike pairs | 6,786 |

## Sources

`_build.json` names the exact lexicon read for each language (`lexicons`), and every row
carries a provenance tag.

| Language | Wordnet | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| en | Princeton WordNet 3.0, in the Open Multilingual Wordnet 1.4 | words, concepts, glosses, examples, every concept relation, en sense antonyms |
| pt | OpenWordnet-PT 1.0.0 | words, their links to concepts, pt sense antonyms; not its definitions or examples, nor its synset relations |
| es | Multilingual Central Repository 3.0 (OMW 1.4) | words, their links to concepts |
| it | MultiWordNet (OMW 1.4) | words, their links to concepts, some glosses |
| fr | WOLF (OMW 1.4) | words, their links to concepts |

Alongside the wordnets:

- **CILI**: the shared concept ids, and an English gloss for a concept WordNet leaves without
  one (none in this build).
- **wordfreq 3.1.1**: word frequencies, from which the sense frequencies and CEFR bands are
  computed. The numbers are derived; wordfreq's word lists are not shipped.
- **Text written for this project**: glosses in es, fr, it and pt, written by an LLM from the
  English gloss and reviewed; look-alike labels and notes; reviewed sense choices, additions and
  drops; example sentences; and the pt inflected forms the build generates from rules of its own,
  with their form notes. These live under `data/curated/` and the build re-applies them.

74,369 of the pt senses are inflected forms (a sense with a form note, such as *anos* on
"year"), almost all generated by rule.
A gloss can exist for a concept with no word in that language, which is why fr and es have a few
more glosses than concepts with a word.

## Provenance

| Tag | Senses | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `omw` | 503,242 | read from the wordnet of the sense's language (own-pt for pt) |
| `inflection` | 73,903 | a pt inflected form generated by rule from its base word |
| `manual` | 756 | a reviewed sense addition |

Concepts are tagged `omw` (117,658), `manual` (66, meanings WordNet lacks, such as the modal
verbs) or `llm` (1, a reviewed gloss repair).

## Measured and estimated values

- **Sense frequency**: 507,445 of 577,901 senses have a token frequency from wordfreq; only
  30,238 are measured at the sense level (single-word English senses, with WordNet's SemCor
  counts to split the word's frequency). The other 547,663 are flagged
  `frequency_is_estimated`.
- **CEFR band**: every sense has one, and every band is estimated (`cefr_is_estimated`): a score
  from the concept's commonness and hypernym depth with a per-language overlay of frequency and
  word length, cut into bands. No graded list is merged in; one (Kelly, CC BY-NC-SA) is used
  only to validate the bands.
  The bands: A1 8,349, A2 15,081, B1 32,208, B2 30,001, C1 34,140, C2 458,122.

## Known gaps

- 8,868 pt concepts have a pt word but no pt gloss: the written glosses cover the concepts the
  previous pt wordnet had.
- es, fr and it have no sense antonyms of their own; they reach opposites through the concept
  antonyms derived from English.
